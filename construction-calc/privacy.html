Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers the construction calculator iPhone app published by Avira LLC ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store or send any personal data to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your phone

Your saved jobs, calculation history and settings are stored only on your device. They stay there until you delete them or delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

How are these construction guides made?

Avira LLC publishes these guides. Avira LLC is the maker of Shim: Construction Calculator, an iPhone app that is coming soon to the App Store. AI helps draft the text. A script computes every number with the same formulas as Shim, and no paid placement appears in any guide.

Who publishes

Avira LLC publishes these pages at devjq.github.io/avira-apps. The pages are plain HTML, with no scripts, no trackers and no ads.

How the pages are written

The text was drafted with AI assistance. Every number in a table is computed by a script that uses the same formulas as Shim. The numbers written in the text were checked against that script's output. Before anything is published, the script checks its calculations against the worked examples in Shim's spec, and it checks each page's title, description and canonical link. If one check fails, nothing is published.

What the checks cover

The worked examples cover slab volume and bag counts, round-column volume, stair risers and stringer length, roof rafter lengths, and how fractions are shown. The checks compare numbers with worked examples. They do not test the pages in a browser or on a phone, and they do not replace the local code, the drawings or the bag you buy.

No paid placements

No company pays for a place in these guides, and no page is an advertisement. The links on a page go to a manufacturer's data sheet, a code section or Shim's page.

Report a mistake

Email avirallcdev@gmail.com with the page address, the number you think is wrong and the value you expected. We check every report.

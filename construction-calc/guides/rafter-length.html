How to calculate rafter length (with overhang and ridge)

On a 6/12 roof with a 12-foot horizontal run, the common rafter line length is 13' 5" (13.416 ft) before any ridge deduction. A 1-foot horizontal overhang adds 1' 1-7/16", for 14' 6-7/16" in all. To get the cut length, deduct half the ridge board's thickness, measured horizontally and multiplied by the rafter factor. For a 1-1/2-inch ridge at 6/12, that is 13/16", which leaves 14' 5-9/16". The numbers assume a horizontal run and overhang, a 1-1/2-inch ridge board, and a common rafter with no hip or valley.

Pitch chart

Rise per foot is the pitch in inches. The rafter factor is √(1 + (pitch ÷ 12)²). Multiply a horizontal length by it to get the length along the rafter. The angle is measured from level.

Worked example: 6/12 roof, 12-ft run, 1-ft overhang, 1-1/2-inch ridge

  1. Pitch. 6/12 means 6 inches of rise per foot of run. The angle is arctan(6 ÷ 12) = 26.57°.
  2. Rafter factor. √(1 + (6 ÷ 12)²) = √1.25 = 1.1180.
  3. Run. 12 ft = 144 in. Line length = 144 × 1.1180 = 160.99 in = 13' 5".
  4. Rise. 144 × 6 ÷ 12 = 72 in = 6' 0".
  5. Overhang add-on. 1 ft = 12 in. 12 × 1.1180 = 13.42 in = 1' 1-7/16".
  6. Line length with overhang. 160.99 + 13.42 = 174.41 in = 14' 6-7/16".
  7. Ridge deduction. 0.75 × 1.1180 = 0.84 in = 13/16".
  8. Cut length. 174.41 − 0.84 = 173.57 in = 14' 5-9/16".

Notes

  • These are common rafter lengths. Hip, valley and jack rafters need their own calculation.
  • Rafter tails, birdsmouth cuts and plumb cuts are not included.
  • If the two sides of a roof differ in pitch or overhang, run each side separately.
  • This is layout geometry, not engineering advice. Rafter size, spacing and span come from the design and your local code.

Which construction math guides are there?

Each guide answers one question a builder types into Google, with worked tables and an example you can follow by hand. The numbers are computed with the same formulas as Shim.

  • How many steps for a 9-foot ceiling? Total rise with floor framing, risers, treads and stringer length.
  • How to calculate rafter length (with overhang and ridge) Pitch chart, line length, overhang and ridge deduction.
  • How many bags of concrete for a 10x10 slab? Cubic yards and bag counts for 60 lb and 80 lb mix.
  • How many bags of concrete per fence post? Concrete per post for 4x4 and 6x6 posts in common hole sizes.

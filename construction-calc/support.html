Support

Need help with the construction calculator, found a wrong result, or want a tool added? Email avirallcdev@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Restoring a purchase

If you bought Pro and it doesn't show on a new phone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Reporting a wrong result

Please include the tool you used, the numbers you entered and the answer you expected. We check every report.

A note on codes

Code limits shown in the app, such as stair rise and run, are general guidance. Always check your local building code and the drawings for your project.

What is Shim: Construction Calculator?

Shim is an iPhone construction calculator for builders, contractors and DIYers. You type in feet, inches and fractions, and it does the job-site math: sums, area, volume, concrete, unit conversion, stairs and roof rafters.

Free

  • Feet, inches and fractions calculator, with fractions to 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32 inch
  • Unit conversion for length, area and volume, imperial and metric
  • Area and volume
  • Concrete for slabs, footings and round columns, with bag counts for 60 lb and 80 lb bags
  • The last 10 results

Nothing free is locked, and there are no ads.

Pro

  • Stairs: risers, treads, total run and stringer length, with the IRC checks
  • Roof pitch and rafters: rise, angle and rafter length, with overhang
  • Saved jobs, with notes
  • Unlimited history

Pro is available as a one-time purchase. No subscription required.

Also

  • Works offline, with no account
  • English and Spanish

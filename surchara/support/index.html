What the app does

Surchara builds one plain, AI-readable page for each product in your store, with a canonical link back to the product page. Shoppers and AI bots get the same page. The app shows which verified AI search and AI training bots read those pages. The paid plan adds a weekly bot report and the orders that came from AI assistants.

Turn on the link

  1. Open Surchara: AI Search Pages from your Shopify admin.
  2. On the Home screen, turn on the "AI product guide link" embed.
  3. The link is added in Shopify's theme editor. You do not need to edit theme code.

The pages are at https://your-store.myshopify.com/apps/ai-guide/, using your store's own address.

Change plan

Open the app, choose Change plan, and pick the Free or the Paid plan. Development stores are not charged. Plan charges are billed by Shopify.

Uninstall

Remove the app from your Shopify admin. We then delete the store's data as described in the privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

This policy covers Surchara: AI Search Pages, a Shopify app published by AVIRA LLC ("we"). It applies to the stores that install the app and to the pages the app builds for them.

Who we are

AVIRA LLC publishes the app. Contact us at avirallcdev@gmail.com.

What we read

  • Product data from the store: titles, descriptions, prices, images and product addresses, which the app turns into one AI-readable page per product.
  • The store's policy pages, such as refund and shipping text, so the pages can show them.
  • On the paid plan, and only with the merchant's grant in Shopify: the order source and referral fields, which show that an order came from an AI assistant.

What we never read or store

We never read or store customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses. The app does not request them from Shopify.

What we store

  • Product snapshots: copies of the product data listed above, used to build the pages.
  • Page requests: the time, the page path, the response status and the user agent. The visitor's IP address is stored only for requests whose user agent names a known bot, such as an AI or search-engine crawler, so that the visit can be checked against the bot operator's published address list. No IP address is stored for other visitors.
  • On the paid plan: order totals and the order source and referral fields.
  • Store records: install and uninstall dates, the plan, and billing totals.

Why we use it

We use this data to build the AI-readable pages, to show which verified AI bots read them, and to make the weekly report and the list of orders that came from AI assistants on the paid plan.

How long we keep it

When a merchant uninstalls the app, Shopify sends us a store-deletion request. We delete the store's data within 48 hours of that request, and in any case no later than 30 days after uninstall. We keep billing totals and visit and order counts for accounting.

Who processes the data

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), region us-east-1 (N. Virginia): runs the app's servers and stores its files.
  • Supabase: hosts the app's database in AWS us-east-1, encrypted at rest.

This policy page loads no trackers, scripts or external fonts.

Data processing terms for merchants

By installing the app, the merchant and AVIRA LLC agree to these terms for the personal data the app processes for the merchant's store.

  • AVIRA LLC processes it on the merchant's behalf, under the merchant's instructions, for the purposes on this page and no others.
  • People at AVIRA LLC who can see it must keep it confidential.
  • It is encrypted in transit and at rest. The subprocessors are AWS and Supabase, listed above. A new subprocessor will be posted on this page before it is used.
  • If a breach affects the merchant's data, AVIRA LLC tells the merchant without undue delay.
  • AVIRA LLC helps the merchant answer customers' requests to see or delete their data, and acts on Shopify's deletion requests.
  • The data is stored in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland rely on the EU standard contractual clauses.

Requests

A merchant or a customer can ask what we hold about a store, or ask us to delete it, by writing to avirallcdev@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

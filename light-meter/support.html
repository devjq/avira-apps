Support

Need help with the light meter, got a reading that looks wrong, or want a feature added? Email avirallcdev@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Restoring a purchase

If you bought Pro and it doesn't show on a new phone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Matching your handheld meter

Every iPhone camera reads light a little differently. If the app's readings differ from a handheld meter you trust, set the calibration offset in Settings to match it.

Reporting a wrong reading

Please include your iPhone model, the film ISO, the reading the app gave and the exposure you expected. We check every report.
